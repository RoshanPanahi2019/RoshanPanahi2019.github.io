About

Bridging construction practice with AI research

Dr. Roshan Panahi is a Postdoctoral Scholar in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at the FAMU-FSU College of Engineering. He focuses on how automated collection, storage, retrieval, and communication of data can improve construction operations and infrastructure resilience.

His current work expands into demolition permitting, post-disaster recovery, and spatiotemporal sustainability methods for debris management. Previously, he applied computer vision and NLP at Skanska and completed his Ph.D. at Oregon State University on automated monitoring of modular construction factories.

He mentors undergraduate researchers through FSU UROP and FAMU EESI, and serves on national committees focused on AI and sensing in civil engineering.

Research interests

  • Automation in construction & modular manufacturing
  • Computer vision & BIM integration
  • Infrastructure resilience & post-disaster recovery
  • Discrete event & agent-based simulation
  • Large language models for AEC workflows
